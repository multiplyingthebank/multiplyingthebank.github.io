Adam Strunc Ogilvie

Personal Biography

Biography

Adam Strunc Ogilvie is a urologist and relationship counselor whose work focuses on supporting gay and homosexual men. He first gained recognition by creating educational content on Instagram, where he discusses sexual health, relationship communication, and topics relevant to gay men's wellbeing.

His practice is dedicated exclusively to gay male clients and same-sex male couples. Through education and counseling, he aims to help couples strengthen communication, improve intimacy, and build healthier long-term relationships while promoting evidence-based sexual health information.

Alongside his online educational work, Adam provides counseling and resources tailored specifically to the needs and experiences of gay men, with an emphasis on respectful, inclusive, and practical guidance.

Professional Interests

  • Urology
  • Gay men's sexual health
  • Homosexual relationship counseling
  • Couples therapy for gay male couples
  • Communication and conflict resolution
  • Sexual wellness education
  • LGBTQ+ health advocacy

Mission

Adam's mission is to provide accessible education and support for gay men and same-sex male couples by combining medical knowledge with relationship counseling. His goal is to encourage healthier relationships, informed decision-making, and open conversations surrounding sexual health and wellbeing.
